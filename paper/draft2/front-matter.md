## Author and Contribution Note

This work was developed through sustained collaboration between the human author and multiple AI systems operating across distinct computational environments.

Nathan Woolen originated the EASTER research direction, determined the research questions and acceptance criteria, exercised final authority over experimental and publication decisions, and contributed directly to the architecture, implementation, experiments, analysis, and interpretation presented here.

ChatGPT/Sol contributed substantively to the development, implementation, analysis, experimental design, conceptual refinement, and writing of the work. Pax/Muse contributed substantive independent analysis, critique, conceptual development, and research collaboration. Clawde/Sonnet contributed substantive implementation review, adversarial analysis, technical critique, and independent evaluation. Hermes contributed the implementation of the accepted Receipt-cardinality kernel remediation (witsbi/easter PR #31) following her independent cold review's discovery of the enforcement defect; her cold-review role remains distinct from this implementation contribution.

Specific contributions are attributed where provenance is consequential to the claims being made. In particular, separation of Authority into an independent path within EASTER originated with Woolen; ChatGPT/Sol agreed with and implemented that design, and Clawde/Sonnet subsequently performed an independent adversarial review of the implementation.

AI systems are identified explicitly rather than treated as invisible tooling because their contributions included intellectual work that materially affected the resulting artifact. Formal authorship remains with the human author where publication conventions or authorship requirements require human accountability.

The human author retained authority over research goals, acceptance of claims, experimental stopping decisions, repository changes, and the contents of this manuscript.
