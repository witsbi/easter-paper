- "EASTER is a blackboard, not a chat room."
- "The kernel holds coordination, never credentials."
- Kernel/userland separation: auth, sessions, transport, refresh, policy are
  userland by design.
- The kernel judges admissibility (structurally valid and live), never wisdom
  (whether a grant sensibly should have been issued).
- Pain-driven design: the ideas come from scar tissue (receptionist billing
  exposure → authority/grants; vendor server pause → distrust of heavy infra;
  threads' normative validator → honest substrate).
