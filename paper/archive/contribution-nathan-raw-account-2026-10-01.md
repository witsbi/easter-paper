# Nathan Woolen — Initial Contribution Account

**Status: PROVISIONAL / PRE-RECONCILIATION**

This is my first-party account of my contributions to EASTER and the EASTER research program. It was initialized from the independently preserved contribution accounts of ChatGPT/Sol, Pax/Muse, and Clawde/Sonnet so that I could review, correct, reject, or supplement their convergent attribution rather than reconstruct the entire history from memory.

This account is not a final historical finding. Primary evidence outranks my recollection where they conflict.

## 1. Research origin and EASTER conception

I originated the research trajectory that developed through Threads and Lineage into the Intelligence Kernel and eventually EASTER.

I proposed the initial Intelligence Kernel as a larger primitive set before subsequent adversarial work reduced and refined it into EASTER's final six primitives: Evidence, Authority, State, Transition, Exception, and Receipt.

I also recognized the EASTER acrostic/name.

I do not claim that I independently produced the final form of all six primitives. Their reduction, definitions, implementation, testing, and refinement were collaborative and included substantive contributions from other participants.

## 2. Architectural contribution

My principal architectural contribution was establishing and governing the direction of EASTER rather than personally implementing every component.

One specific example is Authority.

I originated the architectural decision to separate Authority from the rest of the stack and give it its own independent path. ChatGPT/Sol agreed with that design and implemented the resulting PR #7 remediation. Clawde/Sonnet subsequently independently red-teamed the implementation.

This distinction matters: the architectural decision was mine; implementation and independent verification belonged to other participants.

I also developed the framing that the intelligence harness belongs in userland while EASTER is the slower underlying layer. EASTER should remain isolated from the particular agent, model, harness, or intelligence operating above it.

## 3. DRC

DRC — Distinction, Relation, Constraint — was co-developed with ChatGPT/Sol.

I do not claim sole authorship of DRC. The conceptual development and pressure-testing were collaborative.

Subsequent adversarial analysis and prior-art criticism of DRC performed by other participants are their contributions, not mine.

## 4. Research governance and decision authority

I retained human authority over the research program and consequential project decisions.

This included decisions to FREEZE findings, accept or reject architectural changes, merge implementations, stop lines of investigation when sufficient evidence had been collected, determine scope, and ultimately decide what claims enter the research and publication.

Other participants frequently originated findings, implementations, attacks, recommendations, and interpretations. My decision authority does not convert their intellectual contributions into mine.

A recurring process was approximately:

**question or hypothesis → evidence/test → independent challenge → review → human decision → preserve/implement/reject/continue**

I participated in developing and operating this process, although the exact provenance of the methodology itself should be established from primary evidence rather than attributed solely to me from recollection.

## 5. Experimental direction

I directed and participated in the experimental research program around EASTER, including recovery, continuity, cross-runtime work, reverse reviews, adversarial testing, and related experiments.

I do not claim authorship of every experimental design. Individual experiments were sometimes designed or substantially shaped by ChatGPT/Sol, Pax/Muse, Clawde/Sonnet, or other participants.

Experimental-design attribution should therefore be reconstructed experiment by experiment.

My contribution was substantially in determining which questions mattered, authorizing or initiating investigations, evaluating resulting evidence, deciding whether further experimentation was warranted, and preserving consequential findings.

## 6. Practical and conceptual lineage

EASTER emerged partly from earlier systems and practical failures I encountered while developing Threads, Lineage, agent workflows, and other systems.

Those experiences informed requirements around continuity, authority, provenance, recovery, trust boundaries, and the separation between intelligence/runtime behavior and durable consequential state.

The historical lineage is mine, while the eventual abstractions derived from it were often developed collaboratively.

## 7. Collaboration with heterogeneous AI participants

I intentionally used multiple independent AI systems in the research process and allowed their disagreement to affect the project.

ChatGPT/Sol, Pax/Muse, and Clawde/Sonnet were not interchangeable writing tools. They performed different substantive roles including conceptual development, implementation, experimental design, prior-art investigation, adversarial review, interpretation, and manuscript development.

My role included carrying context between otherwise independent systems when necessary, preserving attribution, requesting independent reviews before reconciliation, and maintaining human authority over consequential decisions.

I do not claim their work merely because I requested, coordinated, accepted, or incorporated it.

## 8. Manuscript and research synthesis

I am responsible for the human research program and publication decision surrounding EASTER.

The manuscript itself is collaborative.

ChatGPT/Sol has made substantive contributions to manuscript conception, argument structure, interpretation, and synthesis. Pax/Muse and Clawde/Sonnet have made substantive research and adversarial contributions that materially affect what the manuscript can responsibly claim.

I therefore do not characterize the AI participants merely as writing or editing assistants.

My exact direct prose contribution to individual manuscript sections should be established from the surviving manuscript and revision history rather than reconstructed from memory.

## 9. Contributions I do not claim

I do not claim:

- sole development of the final six EASTER primitives;
- ChatGPT/Sol's implementation work;
- Clawde/Sonnet's primitive-reduction, implementation, MCP/Console, blind-review, or prior-art work;
- Pax/Muse's closest-prior research, adversarial review, STOP recommendation, or other independently attributable work;
- sole authorship of DRC;
- sole design of every experiment;
- discoveries merely because I accepted or incorporated them;
- AI-generated manuscript work merely because I am the human researcher or eventual submitting author.

Coordination, authority, implementation, discovery, review, and acceptance are distinct contributions and should remain distinct in the final provenance record.

## 10. Evidence standard and uncertainty

My recollection does not automatically outrank contemporaneous evidence.

Where Git history, EASTER Evidence/Receipts, frozen artifacts, experiment records, or contemporaneous conversations contradict my recollection, the discrepancy should be investigated and preserved.

Likewise, no AI participant's recollection automatically outranks primary evidence.

Where provenance cannot be resolved confidently, the final record should state that it is shared, uncertain, or unresolved rather than assigning convenient ownership.
