# Contributing

## Who

Nathan Woolen (author) plus his AI collaborators. Outside contributions are
welcome via pull request but the author keeps every merge decision.

## How

- The manuscript is Markdown (`paper/manuscript.md`). One idea per commit;
  keep commits small and reviewable.
- Thinking that isn't manuscript-ready goes in `paper/notes.md`, not the
  manuscript.
- Don't paste secrets, tokens, hostnames, or instance-specific details into
  the paper or the repo. Publish reusable patterns, not the lab's topology.

## Attribution

Name on the paper, the repo, every talk. Contributions are credited;
authorship stays with the author unless explicitly agreed otherwise.
